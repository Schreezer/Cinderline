# Production traffic fixture

`production-crowd-stall.save` is the end of the measured legal four-player
production march captured before the traffic recovery fix. The match paid for
and produced 100 mobile units and 184 supply per seat, then issued the original
formation goals without combat.

Team 0's Anvil 186 remained at `(909.223, 1097.82)` with goal `(1550, 1294)`,
and Cinderthrow 262 remained at `(847.116, 1122.1)` with goal `(1614, 1422)`.
Both goals had fresh reachable static routes, but friendly traffic left the
orders live indefinitely. The fixture preserves this exact natural state for a
bounded continuation regression.
