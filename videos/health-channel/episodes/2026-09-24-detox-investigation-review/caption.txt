A detox hair straightener? That really appeared in a 2009 investigation.

Young scientists examined 15 detox products and asked their makers what their claims meant and what evidence supported them. The definitions varied, and most offered no evidence.

Before buying a commercial cleanse, ask what it is meant to remove and what reliable research supports that claim. NCCIH reports a lack of compelling research supporting detox diets for toxin removal. The liver and healthy kidneys already process substances and remove waste, though that does not make us immune to poisoning or replace medical care.
