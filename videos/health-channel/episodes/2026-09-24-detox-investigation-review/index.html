In 2009, young scientists
investigated fifteen detox products.
Two questions for their makers.
What does detox mean?
And where's the proof?
No two companies
defined it the same way.
Most offered no evidence.
Oh, and one product?
A hair straightener.
So before buying a cleanse,
ask what it's actually
meant to remove.
Detox diets lack convincing evidence
for removing toxins.
Your liver processes harmful substances;
your kidneys filter waste.
No straightener required.
