In 2009, scientists asked 15 detox brands two questions: what does "detox" mean, and where's the proof? No two gave the same answer. One of the products was a hair straightener. 🧪

Many detox teas contain senna, a stimulant laxative. The scale drop is water and gut contents, not toxins, and it comes back. Your liver and kidneys handle detoxification every day. Ordinary tea is fine to enjoy; the cleansing promise is the part without evidence.
