In 2009, some young scientists
called fifteen detox brands.
Two questions.
What does detox mean?
And where's the proof?
No two brands meant the same thing.
Most had no proof.
Oh, and one of them?
It was a hair straightener.
Detox teas are no different.
Often, it's senna. A laxative.
You lose water, not toxins.
The real detox?
Your liver and kidneys.
No straightener required.
