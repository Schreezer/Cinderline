Natural.
Multigrain.
No added sugar.
They sound healthy... right?
Let's turn the pack around.
Natural sounds reassuring.
But it doesn't tell you
how much sugar, salt,
or saturated fat you're getting.
Multigrain just means more than one grain.
Whole grains?
Not necessarily.
Look for whole wheat
or whole oats in the ingredients.
No added sugar sounds like sugar-free...
but not quite.
Naturally occurring sugars
can still be there.
Check total sugars, too.
Comparing two packs?
Use the same amount...
like per hundred grams.
Then check how much
you actually eat.
The front gets your attention.
The back helps you choose.
Small habit.
Better information.
Flip it before you pick it.
